# Sub-Second Digital Olfaction Purge

To prevent olfactory cross-contamination when switching environmental states (e.g., from Forest to Waterfall), ASEI utilizes a 4-stage active clearing mechanism within the HVAC manifold[cite: 2]. The system relies on piezoelectric mesh atomization (110-130 kHz) creating sub-4µm dry aerosol plumes[cite: 2].

1.  **Pneumatic Back-Suck & Scour:** Micro-pumps instantly reverse liquid away from the mesh aperture, followed by a 100 ms dry $N_2$ scour of the nozzle to prevent oxidation[cite: 2].
2.  **Photocatalytic Oxidation (PCO):** The HVAC return duct pulls ambient air through a $TiO_2$ honeycomb mesh illuminated by 254 nm UV-C LEDs, rapidly cleaving volatile terpenes into odorless $CO_2$ and $H_2O$[cite: 2].
3.  **GAC Bypass:** High-speed pneumatic dampers divert 100% of return air through a virgin Granular Activated Carbon (GAC) bed for 6 to 10 seconds[cite: 2].
4.  **Corona Neutralization:** Ceiling diffusers emit bipolar air ions ($1.5 \times 10^5 \text{ ions/cm}^3$) to aggregate and precipitate any remaining suspended particles[cite: 2].
