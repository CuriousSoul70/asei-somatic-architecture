# Clinical Validation & Testing Methodology

To advance ASEI from a conceptual prototype to an FDA-track Active Implantable Medical Device (AIMD), the mathematical mappings of the Circumplex Model require rigorous ground-truth validation. 

Initial pilot testing and data structuring will utilize the research infrastructure at KIIT University. By leveraging the algorithmic modeling resources at Kinetex Labs, we plan to validate the complex multi-user consensus logic. Furthermore, under the guidance of Dr. Rakesh Mohan Das, the hardware telemetry and signal filtering protocols will be subjected to strict academic scrutiny. Success will be measured by correlating ASEI's environmental shifts with continuous HRV tracking and salivary cortisol reduction baselines over a controlled 8-week trial.
